SOCA – Version 1 (MVP) Scope & Expectations
1. Project Objective
The goal of S⚽️CA Version 1 (V1) is to launch a functional football-focused social platform that enables players to showcase talent, scouts and clubs to discover players, and all users to interact through profiles, content, challenges, and messaging.
2. Core User Roles
SOCA V1 will support the following account types:
• Player
• Scout
• Club
• Organization (e.g. federations, leagues, media brands, governing bodies)

Roles determine permissions and visibility within a single unified platform.
3. Authentication & Onboarding
• Email & password sign-up
• Google sign-in
• Role selection during onboarding
• Profile setup flow per role
4. Profiles & Social Features
Profile Features
• Profile photo & bio
• Location, role, and football-specific metadata
• Public profile pages
• Follower / following system
• Engagement metrics (views, likes)
Content
• Video posts (highlights)
• Public feed visibility
• Like and view tracking
5. Video Upload & Playback
• Video upload for highlight content
• Secure, streamed video playback
• Basic metadata storage (duration, upload date)
6. Discovery & Explore
Player Discovery
• Search and filtering by position, age, location, and role
• Sorted discovery views (latest, featured, popular)
Explore Page
• Featured players
• Trending videos
• Challenges
• Events
7. Challenges & Leaderboards
• Admin-created monthly challenges (e.g. Top Dribble of the Month)
• Player video submissions
• Scoring based on admin input and engagement metrics
• Ranked public leaderboards
8. Messaging
• One-to-one text messaging between users
• Conversation list with unread indicators
9. Events
• Event listings (e.g. trials, showcases, scouting events)
• Event cards displayed on Home / Explore
• Event detail pages (date, location, description, organizer)
• Basic interaction (View / Interested)
10. AI Features (Assistive Intelligence)
SOCA V1 includes an assistive intelligence layer focused on improving discovery, relevance, and matching across the platform.
A. Intelligent Discovery & Recommendations
• Rule-based and behavior-driven recommendation logic
• Ranking influenced by player profile attributes, scout preferences, and engagement signals
B. Personalized Feeds
• Role-aware discovery feeds
• Scout feeds adapt based on interaction patterns
• Player feeds highlight relevant challenges and exposure opportunities
C. Engagement-Based Ranking Signals
• Aggregation of scout interactions, shortlist activity, messaging interest, and challenge performance
11. Admin & Moderation
• Admin management of challenges
• Ability to feature content
• Moderation of reported content
• Manual verification badges (if required)
12. Project Deliverables
The primary deliverable under this engagement is the complete source code for the SOCA mobile application, developed according to the agreed MVP feature scope. The codebase will be delivered via a private GitHub repository, including all necessary project files and basic setup documentation required for a  person to run , extend or maintain the application.
13. Demonstration & Review
Upon completion, the Client will receive a recorded video walkthrough demonstrating the core functionality of the application as implemented in the MVP. This video is intended for review, validation, and presumably stakeholder presentation purposes.
14. Out of Scope Items
14.1 Application Deployment & Distribution
Application deployment, including publishing to the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, TestFlight, or any other distribution platform, is not included in the scope of this engagement. These activities require additional setup, third-party accounts, compliance processes, and review cycles, and may be discussed as a separate engagement if required.
14.2 Hosting, Infrastructure & Ongoing Operations
Ongoing hosting, infrastructure management, monitoring, analytics, and post-delivery operational support are not included in this scope. Responsibility for maintaining, hosting, or operating the application after handover rests with the Client unless otherwise agreed in writing.
15. Ownership & Responsibility
15.1 Account Ownership & Access
All third-party services, platforms, or accounts required to operate the application (including backend services, cloud providers, or developer accounts) are expected to be owned by the Client. The Developer may be granted temporary collaborator access for development purposes and will relinquish access upon project completion.
15.2 Post-Delivery Enhancements
Any requests for additional features, platform support (e.g. web version), deployment assistance, or continued development after delivery of the agreed MVP will be treated as a separate scope of work and subject to additional fees and timelines.
16. Future Phases (Optional Product Roadmap)
The following items represent potential future enhancements and may be discussed as separate paid engagements.
Phase 2 – Social, Video & Launch Expansion
• Stories and short-form reels
• Live streaming capabilities
• Enhanced content moderation tools
• Push notifications
• Media attachments in chat
• Multi-role account switching
• Event ticketing, RSVPs, and monetization
• Optional deployment & store launch support
Phase 3 – Advanced Intelligence & Platform Expansion
• Web platform development (separate browser-based product)
• AI-assisted player comparison and ranking
• Scout preference learning (“Scout Fit Score”)
• Computer vision-based performance analysis
• Live match analysis tools
• Advanced analytics dashboards
• Payments, contracts, transfers, and agent management
17. Final Acceptance
The project shall be considered complete and accepted upon delivery of the source code and demonstration video, and confirmation that the implemented features align with the agreed MVP scope.
